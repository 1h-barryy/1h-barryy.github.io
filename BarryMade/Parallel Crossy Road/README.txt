This is a Crossy Road inspired game. Player need to move through and avoid obstacles. I change the whole theme and the setting to a magical forest  

The control is to use WASD or the arrow keys for movement. The score increase when player reaches new lane ahead. And the game stop once the player hit a obsticals

I used GPT6 Astra. I tend to discuss in chat for overall designs and directions and then write prompt for codex to execute.
