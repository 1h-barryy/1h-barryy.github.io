Overview

In this market reasoning game, you study a market scenario, read technical indicators, and ask an AI analyst for its interpretation. Then you form your own market thesis and compare your prediction with what actually happened.

The AI analyst doesn't just say whether the market will go up or down. It explains the supporting signals, the risks, and the evidence that conflicts.

How it works

  • Study a market scenario
  • Read the technical indicators
  • Ask the AI analyst how it reads the signals, the risks and the conflicts
  • Form your own market thesis and make a prediction
  • Compare your prediction with the actual outcome

Original prototype

The project started as a local prototype built in Python with FastAPI.

Web Edition

I'm now rebuilding it as a Web Edition, with a browser frontend and a separate backend deployed on Render.
